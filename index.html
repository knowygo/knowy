How it works

From “Where are we?” to “Wow, really?”

KnowyGo is designed to make discoveries feel natural during a family journey.

1

Set up a child profile with an age group and interests.

2

As the family travels, the app detects new places along the way.

3

Discover something interesting

4

Keep exploring
