Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

KnowyGo is designed to make family travel educational and engaging by providing children with short, age-appropriate facts about places they are travelling through. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it is shared with service providers, how long we retain it, and how you can request deletion of your information.

1. Information we collect

Account Information: When a parent or adult creates an account using Google Sign-In, we receive and store information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address

This information is used to create and manage your account.

Location Information: The App uses your device's location, with your permission, to identify nearby places and provide relevant travel facts. We access your device's GPS coordinates through the device's location services. The coordinates are used to identify nearby places through the Google Places API. We do not store your GPS coordinates or maintain a location history associated with your account. You can control the App's access to your location through your device settings. If you do not grant location permission, location-based features may not function as intended.

Preferences and Personalisation Information: The App allows the parent or adult user to select:

  • A child's age group
  • The child's interests

The App does not require the child's actual age. These preferences are used to personalise the trivia and generate content that is appropriate for the selected age group and interests.

Saved Content and Learning History: When you choose to read more about a fact, the App may save that fact as part of your learning history. You may also save facts as favourites. These saved facts and your learning history are stored in our database and associated with your account.

Notification Information: The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver trivia notifications. A notification registration token associated with your device may be stored to enable notification delivery. The notification token is deleted when you delete your account.

Analytics Information: The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App, monitor usage, and improve the App's functionality and experience. Firebase Analytics may automatically collect certain information about your device and how you use the App, such as app interactions, device information, and identifiers.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Identify nearby places while you travel.
  • Provide location-based travel facts.
  • Personalise facts based on selected interests and age group.
  • Deliver trivia through notifications.
  • Save your learning history and favourite facts.
  • Understand how the App is used and improve its functionality.
  • Maintain and operate the App.
  • Collect feedback about the App.

3. Location-Based Trivia

When location permission is enabled, the App obtains your current location and uses it to identify nearby places. We send relevant location information to the Google Places API to obtain information about nearby places. We may then use information about the identified place, along with your selected interests and age group, to generate personalised trivia. Your GPS coordinates are not stored as part of your account or used to maintain a history of your movements.

4. AI-Generated Content

The App uses an AI service provided by OpenAI to generate travel trivia. To generate relevant content, the App may send information such as:

  • Information about the identified place or location
  • Selected interests
  • Selected age group
  • Instructions used to generate age-appropriate content

We do not send the child's actual age to the AI service because the App collects an age group rather than an exact age. The AI-generated facts are intended for educational and entertainment purposes and may not always be completely accurate.

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to provide certain App functionality. These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective services.

Google: We use Google services for:

  • Google Sign-In, to authenticate accounts
  • Google Places API, to identify nearby places and provide place information and images

Google may process information in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

Firebase: We use Firebase services to:

  • Authenticate and manage accounts
  • Store account-related information, saved facts, favourites, and learning history
  • Deliver notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Analyse App usage through Firebase Analytics

OpenAI: We use OpenAI's services to generate personalised travel trivia based on information about nearby places and the preferences selected in the App. Third-party services may process information according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Children's Privacy

KnowyGo is designed for parents and adults to use while travelling with children. The parent or adult is the account holder and controls the App's settings and preferences. Children are not required to create accounts or provide their personal information directly to use the App. The App allows the parent or adult to select an age group and interests to personalise the facts presented to children. The App does not require or collect the child's actual age. Parents or guardians who have questions about information associated with their account or wish to request its deletion can contact us using the details provided below.

7. Data Retention

We retain account information and other account-associated data, such as saved facts, favourites, and learning history, for as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality and while your account remains active. We do not retain the user's GPS coordinates as part of their account or maintain a location history. When you delete your account, the information associated with your account is deleted from our systems, including your saved content, learning history, favourites, and notification registration information, subject to any information we may be required to retain by law.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your account through the App's settings options. When you delete your account, we delete the information associated with your account from our systems. If you are unable to delete your account through the App or have questions about the deletion of your information, you can write to us at: knowygo@gmail.com

9. Location and Notification Permissions

The App requests permission to access your device's location to provide location-based trivia. The App also requests notification permission to send travel trivia and other relevant notifications. You can grant, deny, or change these permissions through your device settings.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we process from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our data practices, or applicable requirements. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or wish to request access to or deletion of your information, contact us at:
